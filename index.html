    How to Brew

    1. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard in Settings, then press Start Game. A random recipe appears in the recipe book.
    2. Click ingredients on the shelf, or drag them into the cauldron, to add them. Hover a card to see its numbers. You may add up to 4. Each ingredient has a limited stock.
    3. Watch the Temperature, Density and Magic Power bars. Try to move every marker into its green zone.
    4. Press Brew. Each value may miss its range by up to ±8.
    5. Success: +200 points, +10 s and a new recipe. Failure: you lose 1 life. Used ingredients are spent either way.
    6. The game ends when the time runs out, you lose all 3 lives, or the shelf and the cauldron are both empty.
